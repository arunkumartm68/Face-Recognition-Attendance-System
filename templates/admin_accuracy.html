How it is measured

  • Genuine trials: each photo is matched against all profiles, with that photo left out of its owner's profile. A correct match is the right student under the threshold.
  • Impostor trials: each photo is matched with its owner's profile removed - any match is a false accept.
  • Accuracy = (correct matches + correctly rejected impostors) / all trials.
